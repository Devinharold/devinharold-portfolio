A different era of design leadership.

I'm Devin Harold, Global Director of SmartOps Product Design at Byte by Yum! Brands. My work examines how design leadership changes when AI makes execution abundant and judgment becomes the scarce resource.

Explore my perspective See leadership impact

Making got easier. Knowing what to make did not.

AI collapsed the cost of making and exposed that organizations were never limited by production capacity, but by how well they decide. The shift isn't a new tool in the stack but instead a rethink of how design organizations operate.

I embed AI into research, product strategy, design execution, and organizational decision-making, so teams move faster, learn continuously, and create more business value. The future of design leadership isn't about producing more screens but about building intelligent organizations where human judgment and AI improve how products are imagined, validated, and ultimately delivered.

The discipline is what you do with the capacity AI frees: treat it as investment capital by reinvesting in strategy, paying down UX debt, or deepening business impact rather than being absorbed by default as more output.

AI should increase the quality of judgment, not the volume of output.

I build AI-native design organizations that turn human insight into measurable business performance — AI as infrastructure for judgment, people accountable for context, intent, and consequence.

When production gets cheap, the cost of a wrong decision goes up: a team can now build the wrong thing faster — and more convincingly — than ever before.

The real leverage is compressing the distance between a question and the evidence that answers it, so more decisions get made on evidence instead of seniority or opinion.
